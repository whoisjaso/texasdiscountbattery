# Texas Discount Battery Co. Design System

## Direction

Premium roadside utility. The site should feel fast, practical, and trustworthy: a Houston battery shop that gives a clear answer, a clear price, and a quick install.

## Audience

Mobile-first drivers near Southeast Houston and Hobby Airport who need a battery now, want a fair price, and do not want confusing repair-shop language.

## Conversion Job

Drive two actions:

1. Call the shop.
2. Get directions to 7423 Airport Blvd.

The `$65` battery option should be visible early without implying every battery is the same price.

## Visual Rules

- Use the repaired transparent header logo: `assets/logo-nav-header-clean.png`.
- Stay in the dark Texas red, blue, graphite, and silver system.
- Avoid white page surfaces, cream backgrounds, decorative blobs, and generic gradient panels.
- Keep cards at 8px radius or less.
- Center section headings and major offer language.
- Do not use oversized type inside constrained cards.
- Make mobile controls large, direct, and thumb reachable.
- Hero battery visual should be a playful interactive 3D model, not a flat realistic product photo.
- Mobile action dock should use strong dark surfaces and shaped controls; avoid cheap-looking button gradients.

## Copy Rules

- Short sentences.
- Concrete service language.
- No invented performance metrics.
- Use real review language only where reviews already exist.
- Prefer "tested", "installed", "free install with purchase", "near Hobby Airport", and "7423 Airport Blvd".

## Motion Rules

- Manual scrolling stays native.
- Anchor navigation may animate smoothly.
- Reviews auto-scroll continuously and pause on hover or focus.
- The hero battery model may subtly rotate and respond to pointer movement without blocking native vertical scrolling.
- Motion must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
